# Experimental 1 checkpoint audit

Audited for release preparation on20 October2026. These are completed research
states with reproducible development evidence. They are not a completed M1
version, a production model or an independent final qualification.

The selected extraction assembly retains its actual evaluated TYPE14, LOC13,
original66, POL/REL96 and BIND83 tensors. Its separate canonicalizer is the
completed CAN32 fixed16-pass state evaluated as a CAN-only replacement on the
same368 raw development records. No newer TYPE, localization, binding,
polarity or relation experiment is silently mixed into this selection.

The slot encoder is retained for reproducibility. Text-to-event inference and
canonical annotation do not require its application-specific consumer path.
The original research persistence envelope duplicated CAN28 tensors and
contained local paths and research custody metadata. It is not distributed.
Each trained tensor is exported once through the public data-only FP32
safetensors bundle format. The generic format change does not change tensor
values. Loading requires an independently obtained top-manifest SHA256.

The newer experiments were reviewed separately; none replaces the frozen
selection for this package. As of this audit, no research job is asserted to
be running merely because a historical launch record exists.

The main public five-group API remains unchanged. Additive CAN128 and CAN32
owners preserve the selected native arithmetic. CAN32 chooses either full
raw context or one ASCII blank on the left, using its trained router, before
one ordinary byte decode. The original CAN96 factory remains available.
No catalogue lookup or symbolic correction supplies predicted IDs.
`canonical_inventory27` describes membership of an output in the declared
finite inventory; it never changes, filters or replaces the output string.

The exact raw development results and denominators are documented in the
[Experimental1 model card](EXPERIMENTAL_1.md). The same extractor produces
553 events for704 Gold events on368 records. Development performance remains
well below the completion criteria. CAN's1720/1720 full-Gold-mention component
control supplies the correct mention spans and is not a full pipeline score.
Consumer results use an additional frozen research consumer; it is not needed
or included in this text-to-JSON package.

Verification includes all308 trained tensor values against their authenticated
source archives and strict public restoration. Two original physical batches
of8 saved feature inputs, one English and one Russian, cover predictedE0,
E1,E2 andE3. Without Gold inputs or new donor calls, restored public inference
matches99,288 TYPE score values,64 localization arrays, two original66 score
banks,14 POL/REL arrays and144 saved binding-potential arrays bit for bit.
HEAD/type, polarity, directed relations and native beam choices agree.
CAN's52 predicted mentions have identical native/public route logits, routes
and output bytes. This checks preservation on the fixed batches, not general
semantic correctness or invariance under arbitrary batch changes.

The neural components were trained in the project. Available training
provenance describes generated synthetic lexical, sentence-template and byte
transduction examples; no external pretrained M1/CAN checkpoint is bundled.
The CAN32 ancestry retains the project's learned24-tensor byte transducer,
then trains its local scorer and router. These records establish the observed
provenance, not an independent legal certification of every historical input.
No training corpus, private research report, personal path, secret or donor
weight is included in the release package. The feature donor has separate
source and licensing requirements described in the provider audit.
